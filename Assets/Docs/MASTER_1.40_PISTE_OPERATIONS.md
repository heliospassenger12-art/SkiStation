# SkiStation 1.40 — Piste Operations & Grooming Fleet

1.40 turns piste maintenance into an operational system.

- Real grooming fleet with snowcats and service vehicles.
- Finite crew capacity and active jobs.
- Manual grooming and heavy grooming.
- Night preparation window before opening.
- Prioritization of degraded pistes.
- Maintenance expenses recorded through FinanceRuntime.
- Fuel/availability state for grooming vehicles.
- F6 operations panel.
- Morning/domain preparation score.

The system remains lightweight and management-level: it does not replace skier movement or route authority.
