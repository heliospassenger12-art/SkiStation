# SkiStation 1.45 — Sécurité & Pisteurs

## Philosophy
Realistic enough to create believable decisions, but deliberately not suffocating.

## Incidents
- Minor: quick response, one patrol team.
- Moderate: normal rescue response.
- Serious: several resources may be required; helicopter can be requested.
- Critical: helicopter may be required and response is longer if unavailable.

## Lift protection
Aerial lift pylons automatically receive protective cushions. They have no maintenance, stock or placement management in this prototype; they simply reduce the probability of a serious collision becoming severe.

## Player modes
Simplified mode keeps the system lightweight. Advanced conditions can increase incident risk through ice/soup and traffic, while the player still manages teams rather than individual rescuers.

## UI
F7 opens the safety panel.
