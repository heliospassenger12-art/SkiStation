SkiStation 2.33 - Combo HUD and validated full-spin boost rewards

- The previously optional combo HUD is now attached to the test avatar.
- Displays the active successful-figure chain, reward multiplier and time left.
- Boost requires a completed near-full aerial rotation (>=300 degrees total)
  and aligned landing (<=48 degrees residual), not a small partial turn.
- Crashes and expired 12-second windows reset the chain.
- Scene menu: SkiStation > Arcade > Creer et ouvrir la scene de glisse 2.33.
- Prior versions 2.14-2.32 and this version remain UNTESTED in Unity.
- This is still an isolated prototype, not integrated into the management scene.
